About Blackspyre

Blackspyre Investments is a Los Angeles-based private investment fund that pools capital from a small, personally known group of investors and manages it with discipline, transparency, and close attention to risk.

Who we are

Blackspyre Investments is a pooled investment vehicle founded by Binh Do, Ivan Huang, and Ethan Lu. We manage capital on behalf of a deliberately small group of investors, people we know personally and have a direct relationship with.

Because we stay small, every investor gets direct access to the people managing their money, a clear view of how the fund is positioned, and straightforward reporting on how it is performing.

How we work for our investors

Our aim is to grow our investors' capital over time. We do that in three ways.

Pooled capital

Investor funds are combined into one fund and invested under a single, consistent strategy. Pooling gives clients access to opportunities and portfolio construction that are harder to replicate individually.

Active, disciplined management

The founders make investment decisions on behalf of the fund, with risk management built into how positions are sized and monitored. The goal is to pursue returns without taking on risk the investors have not signed up for.

Alignment and transparency

Our clients are our relationships, not anonymous accounts. Investors can securely log in to see how their capital is performing in the fund, so they are never guessing where they stand.

Our story

We met in college, where we discovered a shared desire to help grow wealth for friends and colleagues within our respective fields.

A spire stands tall, holds its ground, and can be seen from a distance. We chose the name Blackspyre because it reflects how we want to manage your capital: with discipline, steadiness, and full transparency.
